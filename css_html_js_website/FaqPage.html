Frequently Asked Questions

  • Q: What is Coding Ninjas?

    A: Coding Ninjas is a company renowned for its diverse array of clothing and weaponry options, perfect for tech enthusiasts looking to debug their style and execute their preferences.

  • Q: How do I create an account?

    A: At Coding Ninjas, we believe in keeping things as anonymous as a ninja! That's why we don't require user accounts. You can browse our shop and make purchases without the need to create an account. We value your privacy and convenience.

  • Q: How can I place an order?

    A: Placing an order is easy! Simply go to your desired page Clothing or Weapons, click "Add to Cart" on every item you want. Once you're ready, click "Checkout" to complete your purchase.

  • Q: What payment methods do you accept?

    A: We accept various payment methods through crypto currency, including bitcoin, monero, etherium, and more. You can choose your preferred payment method during checkout.

  • Q: How long will it take to receive my order?

    A: The delivery time depends on your location and the shipping method you choose at checkout. We provide estimated delivery times for each option during the checkout process.
